# 60-Second Elevator Pitch Script
**Speaker:** Indigo Collier | Phase 1 Showcase — Persevere

---

## The Script

**[INTRODUCTION]**
Hi, I'm Indigo Collier — a software developer based in Memphis, Tennessee.

**[BACKGROUND]**
I completed Phase 1 of the Persevere Full Stack Developer Program, where I built a four-page responsive portfolio from scratch — working through HTML, CSS, Flexbox, Grid, accessibility, performance optimization, and Tailwind CSS over 20 days.

**[SKILLS]**
My portfolio scores 99 to 100 on Lighthouse performance and above 94 on accessibility across every page. I write semantic HTML, use ARIA labels, and optimize for real users — not just passing a checklist.

**[PROOF]**
The projects page showcases six real things I have shipped — including Lo and Lady Labs, a React puppy adoption platform, Rescue Tennessee, a donation platform for dog rescues across the state, and Soul Study, an AI-powered study tool built with Next.js and Claude.

**[GOALS]**
Outside of Persevere I am currently working as a software developer at Banyan Labs building production applications. Phase 2 is next — and I am ready for JavaScript.

---

## Timing

Approximately 60 seconds at a natural conversational pace.

---

## Delivery Notes

- Speak slower than feels natural — nervous speakers always rush
- Pause briefly after naming each project — let them land
- "I am ready for JavaScript" should sound confident, not trail off
- Practice out loud at least 8 times before the showcase
- It reads differently than it sounds — always practice spoken, not silently

---

## Q&A Prep

**"What was the hardest thing you built?"**
The contact form — getting the fieldset/legend accessibility pattern semantically correct and hitting zero W3C validation errors required real attention to detail.

**"What is Tailwind CSS?"**
A utility-first CSS framework where you style elements by adding classes directly in HTML instead of writing a separate stylesheet. My pages scored 100 on Lighthouse performance after converting to Tailwind.

**"What are you building at Banyan Labs?"**
Production applications in Next.js, TypeScript, and Firebase — shipping features to real users on a professional team every week.

**"What do you want from Phase 2?"**
JavaScript — specifically how to add interactivity and logic to the interfaces I already know how to build.
